The nodes you actually want to manage get their catalogs from the compiler, and run Puppet on a schedule.

**What you'll learn:**

* Adding agents with `ovadm::add_agent`, pointed at a compiler
* Managing the agent with [openvox_agent](https://forge.puppet.com/modules/miharp/openvox_agent)
* Turning on the agent service for every node in `data/common.yaml`
